Website privacy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This notice covers this website only. Each of our apps has its own privacy policy, listed on the Apps page.

Who is responsible

X Dev & Security Solution SRL, Romania (“we”). You can reach us at contact@xdss.net. Our company details are on the Company information page.

What this website collects

Nothing directly. The site has no forms, no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. It loads no fonts, scripts or images from other websites.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc. When you visit, GitHub's servers receive technical data such as your IP address and browser type, which GitHub may log to keep the service secure. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

If you email us

We use your email address and what you write only to answer you, and we keep the conversation only as long as needed for that and for our legal obligations.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, and you can complain to the Romanian data protection authority (ANSPDCP).

What we make

Puzzle games

Sudoku, nonograms, minesweeper, mahjong solitaire and a traffic escape puzzle. Logic puzzles you can solve by reasoning, never by guessing, with big readable boards.

Everyday tools

A focus timer, an expense log, a water reminder, a QR code scanner and a notes app. Each one does one job well, in as few taps as possible.

How we build them

No account needed

You never create an account. What you make in our apps is kept on your phone, not on our servers.

Every app has its own privacy policy that says exactly what is collected and why. You can read them all on the Apps page.

Fair pricing

Our apps are free to download. Some show ads and offer a one-time purchase that removes them. No subscriptions.
